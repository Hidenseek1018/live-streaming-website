Seller & Sales Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. These are standard terms for this site. They are not legal advice — please have a lawyer review them before real money moves through the site.

1. How sales work here

Anyone 18 or older can list a product — from the Studio while live, from a stream room, or from the Dashboard. A listing can be pinned to a live stream or listed on its own. When a buyer checks out, the site records the order: the product, the price, the buyer's name, and how it was paid for.

Card payments are processed securely by Stripe. When card checkout is switched on, the buyer is taken to Stripe's payment page and the money goes to the site's Stripe account. While the site is in test mode, payments use Stripe's test system and no real money moves; when live payments are switched on, real charges apply. Card details are never stored on this site. When card checkout is off, no money moves through the site at all — the order is only a record, and any payment happens entirely between the buyer and the seller, outside this site, at their own risk.

2. Refunds — what the site can and cannot do

If you paid by card through Stripe and something goes wrong with your order, report the problem — from the stream room or the Dashboard, with your order number and what went wrong (not as described, never arrived, damaged, or something else). The site owner reviews every report and may refund a paid card order through Stripe. A refund goes back to the original card and usually takes a few days to appear on your statement. Refunds are for the full order amount only.

The site does not hold money for anyone — there is no escrow. If your order was record-only (no card payment through the site), the site has no payment to refund, and any refund is entirely between you and the seller. The site does not guarantee that a seller will deliver or that a buyer will pay.

3. Seller responsibilities

  • Describe your products honestly — what they are, their condition, the price, and how many you have.
  • Only list things you can actually provide, and keep your stock count up to date. Each recorded order reduces the stock shown.
  • You are responsible for delivering what you sold and for any payment you arrange with the buyer.
  • If a paid order is refunded because the sale went wrong, the buyer gets their money back — you keep nothing from a refunded sale.
  • Do not list anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit, or dangerous, and do not list anything you do not have the right to sell.
  • Repeated problem reports or listing reports against you can get your channel blocked.
  • You must be 18 or older to sell, and you agree to these terms when you tick the seller acknowledgment box on a listing.

4. Prices and stock

Prices are shown in US dollars as listed by the seller. Stock is the number the seller entered, minus recorded orders. The site does not set prices, take a cut, or hold inventory.

5. Disputes

If something goes wrong with a sale, report the problem from the stream room or the Dashboard with your order number. The site owner reviews every report. For a paid card order, the owner may refund the order through Stripe (see section 2). For a record-only order, the site has no payment to refund. The site does not otherwise mediate disputes and cannot force a seller to deliver or a buyer to pay. Repeated reports against a seller can get their channel blocked.

6. Listings can be reported and removed

Anyone can report a listing they think is misleading, prohibited, or a scam — use the Report button on the listing. The site owner reviews reports and may remove a listing, end a stream, or block a channel at any time — for example, for misleading listings, prohibited items, or abuse. Removal does not cancel any arrangement you already made with a buyer or seller outside the site, and it does not by itself refund an order.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The version on this page is the one that applies.
