Seller & Sales Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. These are standard terms for this site. They are not legal advice — please have a lawyer review them before real money moves through the site.

1. How sales work here

Anyone 18 or older can list a product — from the Studio while live, from a stream room, or from the Dashboard. A listing can be pinned to a live stream or listed on its own. When a buyer checks out, the site records the order: the product, the price, the buyer's name, and the payment method the buyer chose.

No money moves through this site. There is no payment processor, no card is charged, and the site holds no money for anyone. If the buyer pays the seller, that payment happens entirely between the two of them, outside this site, at their own risk.

2. No buyer protection, no refunds through the site

This site provides no buyer protection, no escrow, and no refunds. An order recorded here is only a record — it is not a payment, and it does not guarantee the buyer will pay or the seller will deliver. Refunds and returns are entirely between the buyer and the seller. If you buy something, make sure you trust the seller and agree on payment and delivery with them directly before you pay anything.

3. Seller responsibilities

  • Describe your products honestly — what they are, their condition, the price, and how many you have.
  • Only list things you can actually provide, and keep your stock count up to date. Each recorded order reduces the stock shown.
  • You are responsible for delivering what you sold and for any payment you arrange with the buyer.
  • Do not list anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit, or dangerous, and do not list anything you do not have the right to sell.
  • You must be 18 or older to sell, and you agree to these terms when you tick the seller acknowledgment box on a listing.

4. Prices and stock

Prices are shown in US dollars as listed by the seller. Stock is the number the seller entered, minus recorded orders. The site does not set prices, take a cut, or hold inventory.

5. Disputes

The site does not mediate disputes between buyers and sellers and cannot force a refund, a delivery, or a payment. If something goes wrong with a sale, contact the other person directly. The site owner may remove a listing or block a channel, but that does not resolve a payment or delivery dispute.

6. Listings can be removed

The site owner may remove any listing, end any stream, or block any channel at any time — for example, for misleading listings, prohibited items, or abuse. Removal does not cancel any arrangement you already made with a buyer or seller outside the site.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The version on this page is the one that applies.
