How selling works

1. Sign up below with your channel name and how you want to be paid.

2. The site owner approves you. Only approved sellers can list a product or run a break — that's how the marketplace stays honest.

3. List products or run a break from the Studio or your stream room, and go live.

4. Buyers pay securely at checkout. Sales are paid to the site owner's account first, and the owner pays you out using the payout details you gave here.

You must be 18 or older to sell. While the site is in test mode, checkouts use test payments only — no real money moves until live payments are switched on.
