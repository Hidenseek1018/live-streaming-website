Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. These are standard terms for this site. They are not legal advice — please have a lawyer review them before real money moves through the site.

1. What we collect

  • Visitor counts: every page load is counted. We store a random visitor ID in a cookie (visitor_id, 1 year) so we can count unique visitors. We do not store IP addresses.
  • Age check: when you enter your date of birth, the server checks it and issues a random verification token (cookie age_verified, 30 days). Your date of birth itself is never stored.
  • Display name and viewer ID: a nickname and a random viewer ID are stored in your own browser (localStorage) so chat and likes work. There are no accounts.
  • Streams, chat, and follows: stream titles, channel names, chat messages, likes, and follows are stored on the server so the site works.
  • Orders and products: product listings and orders (buyer name, product, amount, payment method chosen) are stored on the server. Saved payment methods store only a nickname, the kind (Card, PayPal, etc.), and the last 4 digits or handle — never full card numbers.
  • Admin: the admin passcode is kept in your browser session only while the admin panel is unlocked.

2. What we do NOT collect

No IP addresses, no location, no device fingerprinting, no advertising trackers, and no third-party analytics. There are no ads on this site, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone.

3. Cookies and browser storage

  • visitor_id — random ID for visitor counts, 1 year.
  • age_verified — random token proving you passed the 18+ check, 30 days, HttpOnly.
  • localStorage viewer_id, display_name, age_verified_until — your nickname and verification reminder, kept in your browser.
  • sessionStorage admin_code — only on the admin panel, cleared when you lock it or close the tab.

Clearing your browser's cookies and site data resets all of these — you will get a new visitor ID and nickname, and will need to verify your age again.

4. How your information is used

Only to run the site: showing streams and chat, counting visitors, recording orders, and letting the site owner moderate (remove streams, messages, or listings, and block channels). Nothing is used for advertising or sold to anyone.

5. Where it is stored

On the site's own server, in its database. The site owner (and the admin panel) can see streams, chat, orders, listings, and visitor counts.

6. Children

This site is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and under-18 visitors are blocked by the age check.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The version on this page is the one that applies.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the site owner through the site.
